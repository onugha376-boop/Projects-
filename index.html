Fried Rice

The perfect combination of ingredients to savor your rice meal in the best way ever.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Curry Powder
  • Spice Seasoning
  • Red Kidney Beans
  • Carrots
  • Sweet Corn
  • Sausage
  • Green Pepper

Instructions

  1. Wash your rice and boil(till desired texture) with curry powder for yellow coloring.
  2. Cut your carrots into cubes.
  3. Cut your sausages similar to your carrots, do same to your green pepper.
  4. Fry the sausage a little then add your boiled rice and mix gently.
  5. Gradually add your carrots, your red kidney beans then your sweet corn.
  6. Mix gently until uniform then cook at low fire until water dries up.

Better than takeout! Stop waiting for delivery and make this 20-minute, high-heat dish at home with any kind of rice for the best texture. It’s always a good time. It's "fried rice o'clock"—perfect for Sunday enjoyment and a total mood-booster.
